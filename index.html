Geospatial Product Evidence (GPE) Vocabulary

Version 1.0 · issued 2026-09-02

Canonical namespace: https://maple-feng.github.io/gpe/#

Standards-aligned project vocabulary for claim-conditioned geospatial product evidence, obligations, and validation. This vocabulary does not claim ISO conformance.

Machine-readable resources

  • RDF/Turtle vocabulary
  • JSON-LD 1.1 context

Core model

The vocabulary defines ProductClaim, ProductRelease, ProcessingActivity, SourceAsset, ValidationAssessment, QualityEvidence, EvidenceRecord, RequirementDefinition, claim types, activation classes, applicability/evidence states, and evidence-path status resources.

Fragment term IRIs such as #ProductClaim dereference to this landing page. Machine-readable definitions are provided by the linked Turtle vocabulary and JSON-LD context.

Maintained in Maple-feng/gpe.
